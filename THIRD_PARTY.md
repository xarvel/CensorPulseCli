## Data the client can use at run time (not distributed here)

- **GeoLite2** ASN and country CSV tables, fetched by `cpprobe geoip update`
  or `scripts/geoip-update.sh` (same URL) from the `release` branch of
  [Loyalsoldier/geoip](https://github.com/Loyalsoldier/geoip). The tables are
  MaxMind GeoLite2 data, redistributed under that project's terms; the
  Loyalsoldier/geoip repository itself is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (its code
  additionally GPL-3.0). The probe reads the tables locally and sends nothing
  to MaxMind or anyone else. Check the
  [GeoLite2 licence](https://www.maxmind.com/en/geolite2/eula) before
  redistributing reports that embed the lookups.

## Research the tests follow

- [hyperion-cs/dpi-checkers](https://github.com/hyperion-cs/dpi-checkers) and
  the write-ups by its author on the tcp 16-20 / l4-25 rule
  ([net4people/bbs#490](https://github.com/net4people/bbs/issues/490)) and the
  TLS handshake rate rule ("siberian",
  [habr.com/ru/articles/1044396](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1044396/)):
  `tls.burst`, `tcp.packets`, `udp.packets`, the lenient parrots in
  `tls.fingerprint`. See `docs/design/prior-art-dpi-checkers.md`.
- [Runnin4ik/dpi-detector](https://github.com/Runnin4ik/dpi-detector): the
  long-flow cut test, SNI allow-list search, RTT-adaptive timeouts. See
  `docs/design/prior-art-dpi-detector.md`.
- Tor blocking in Russia: OONI (2021-12), ntc.party threads 1477 / 8006 /
  14188, net4people/bbs #97, #422, #603, Bocovich et al., "Snowflake"
  (USENIX Security 2024), the covertDTLS paper (FOCI 2025). See
  `docs/design/tor-blocking-ru.md`.
- uTLS parrots: [refraction-networking/utls](https://github.com/refraction-networking/utls).
